About

A programmer who likes the messy middle

I'm a Unity developer based in Chennai. Most of my work lives in the middle of a game's life — after the idea, before the polish, where a mechanic either holds up under a hundred playtests or quietly falls apart.

At HFG Entertainments I spent a year and a half on mobile titles: gameplay systems, puzzle and mini-game design, third-party SDKs that never behave, and the profiling that keeps a game running on phones that were never built for it. Fifteen games shipped, and somewhere past a hundred puzzles and mini-games along the way.

My own projects are where I chase the systems I don't know how to build yet — a 2D platformer, a 3D endless runner, a first-person escape game. Different genres on purpose. Each one started as a question I couldn't answer on paper.

These days most of my time goes into something much bigger: an unannounced retail sim made under my studio, Primeva Entertainment. It's the most ambitious thing I've built and the least I can say about it — so there's a page for that.

Away from the keyboard I throw shot put and play tennikoit. State level, a while back — and still the reason I'm stubborn about reps.

What I'm good at

  • Gameplay systems that stay readable once the game gets big
  • Getting a mobile build to a frame rate you can actually ship
  • Dropping a third-party SDK in without breaking everything else
  • Working with design, art and QA instead of around them
  • Finishing things — the unglamorous part most people skip
